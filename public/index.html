    Looking back

    Four questions I hear from owners.

    Would you have made the upgrade?

    Some upgraders moved up and gained. Others held a flat that did the same job for less. I compare the two paths on your own cash, CPF and loan numbers.

    Would you have picked another district?

    The famous address and the best-performing address are often different projects. I check what each district's projects actually resold for.

    Would you have bought more bedrooms?

    A bigger home at a lower price per sqft has beaten smaller units at a premium in the cases below. I test this by bedroom type, not by project average.

    Would you have bought in a different year?

    Entry timing and entry price explain much of the spread in outcomes. The chart above is the backdrop for that decision.

    My parents' en bloc

    In 2018 my parents' estate signed an en bloc sale: 43 units, $95 million, to a developer. The buyer later called off the deal and the owners went to court. In November 2021 the High Court ruled for the buyer and the deposit went back. One clause in the agreement required planning permission for at least 120 units, and the authority's limit for the site was 112. I watched a transaction that size turn on a single number, and it made me want to understand where numbers like that come from.
