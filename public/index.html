The problem

Most buyers get stuck on the choice.

New launch or resale. East or Central. 2-bedroom or 3-bedroom. Every option has a good argument, so people wait, and the market moves anyway. Budget is rarely what holds them back. Decision paralysis is.

How I look at it

Three ideas behind every recommendation.

Property is capital

A home is the biggest asset most people will hold. The question is whether that capital is working for you.

Exit matters more than entry

Who will realistically buy this from you in 5 to 10 years, and at what price? I start from the exit and work backwards.

You have more than one cycle

If you are young, you will likely go through 2 or 3 property cycles. A decision you can restructure later deserves less fear.
